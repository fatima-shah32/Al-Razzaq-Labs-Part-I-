# Introduction to AI Concepts

## Task 1: Key AI Milestones in History

### Important Milestones

- 1950: Alan Turing proposed the Turing Test.
- 1955: Logic Theorist became the first AI program.
- 1956: Dartmouth Conference officially established Artificial Intelligence as a field.
- 1997: IBM Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
- 2011: IBM Watson won the Jeopardy quiz competition.
- 2016: DeepMind AlphaGo defeated Go champion Lee Sedol.
- Present Day: Deep Learning, Generative AI, and Large Language Models are transforming industries.

## Task 3: AI Importance Summary

Artificial Intelligence is transforming modern society by enabling machines to perform tasks that traditionally require human intelligence. AI improves efficiency, accuracy, and decision-making across healthcare, finance, transportation, education, and customer service. Technologies such as machine learning, neural networks, and deep learning allow systems to learn from data and continuously improve. In the future, AI is expected to drive innovation, automate complex processes, and solve global challenges, making it one of the most important technologies of the 21st century.
