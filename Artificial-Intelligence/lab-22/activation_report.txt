Lab 22: Intro to Activation Functions

Dataset: Iris
Model: Simple MLP

Activation Function Results:
Activation Function  Test Loss  Test Accuracy
            sigmoid     0.1121         0.9667
               tanh     0.0322         1.0000
               relu     0.0211         1.0000
         leaky_relu     0.0198         1.0000

Observations:
Sigmoid can suffer from vanishing gradients.
Tanh usually performs better than sigmoid.
ReLU often converges faster in neural networks.
Leaky ReLU helps avoid the dying ReLU problem.
