Lab 18: Text Generation with RNNs

Dataset: Small Alice-inspired text corpus
Text Length: 291 characters
Unique Characters: 23
Sequence Length: 40
Number of Sequences: 251

Model Architecture:
LSTM(128) -> Dense(vocabulary size, softmax)

Generated Text:
 alice was beginning to get very tired o  e   e   e   e   e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e    e 

Observation:
Character-level RNNs can learn local character patterns but may generate repetitive or imperfect text on small datasets.
