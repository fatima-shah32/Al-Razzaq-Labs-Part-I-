Lab 17: Exploring GRU Networks

Dataset: Synthetic noisy sine wave time-series dataset
Sequence Length: 50

Model Comparison:
GRU Test Loss: 0.002369
LSTM Test Loss: 0.001797
GRU Training Time: 18.10 seconds
LSTM Training Time: 16.30 seconds

Observation:
GRU networks are simpler than LSTM networks because they use fewer gates.
GRU models can train faster while still performing well on sequence data.
LSTM models may perform better on longer or more complex sequences.
