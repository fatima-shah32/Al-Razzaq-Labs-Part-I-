Lab 22: Batch Normalization in Deep Networks

Dataset: MNIST
Model Type: Feedforward Neural Network

Comparison Results:
Accuracy with Batch Normalization: 0.9733
Accuracy without Batch Normalization: 0.9746
Training Time with Batch Normalization: 30.80 seconds
Training Time without Batch Normalization: 26.94 seconds

Observation:
Batch Normalization stabilizes training by normalizing layer activations.
It can improve convergence speed and help the model generalize better.
Performance may vary depending on dataset, architecture, and training settings.
