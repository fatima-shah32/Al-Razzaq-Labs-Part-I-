Lab 23: Experimenting with Different Optimizers

Dataset: MNIST
Optimizers Compared: SGD, Adam, RMSprop

Optimizer  Test_Loss  Test_Accuracy  Final_Train_Accuracy  Final_Validation_Accuracy
      SGD   0.333959         0.9072              0.900000                     0.9072
     Adam   0.091007         0.9716              0.978900                     0.9716
  RMSprop   0.085646         0.9738              0.978917                     0.9738

Observation:
SGD is simple but may converge slower.
Adam adapts learning rates and often performs well quickly.
RMSprop is also adaptive and commonly works well for sequence-based tasks.
