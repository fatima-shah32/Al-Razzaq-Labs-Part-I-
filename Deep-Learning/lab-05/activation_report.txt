Lab 05: Exploring Activation Functions

Activation functions tested: ReLU, Sigmoid, Tanh
Dataset: Random synthetic dataset
Model: Small feedforward neural network

Final Loss Values:
ReLU Final Loss: 0.0944
Sigmoid Final Loss: 0.1356
Tanh Final Loss: 0.1374

Observation:
ReLU usually learns quickly and is commonly used in hidden layers.
Sigmoid can be useful but may suffer from vanishing gradients.
Tanh centers outputs around zero and can converge better than sigmoid in some cases.
