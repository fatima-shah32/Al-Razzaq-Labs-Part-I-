Lab 24: Custom Loss Functions in Keras

Custom loss implemented:
custom_mse = mean(square(y_true - y_pred))

Dataset: Synthetic regression dataset
Model: Dense neural network

Results:
       Model  Validation_Loss  Validation_MAE
  Custom MSE         0.095587        0.264455
Built-in MSE         0.097193        0.268230

Observation:
Custom loss functions allow flexible model training for specific problems.
The custom MSE behaved similarly to built-in MSE because both compute mean squared error.
