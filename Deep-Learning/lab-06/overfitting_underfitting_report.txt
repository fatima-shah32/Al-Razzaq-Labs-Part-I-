Lab 06: Overfitting and Underfitting

Dataset: California Housing
Reason: load_boston is removed in newer scikit-learn versions.

Linear Regression Training Loss: 0.5179
Linear Regression Validation Loss: 0.5559

Interpretation:
Overfitting occurs when training loss is low but validation loss is high.
Underfitting occurs when both training and validation losses are high.
Dropout can help reduce overfitting in neural networks.
